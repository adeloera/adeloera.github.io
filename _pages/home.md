### About Me

I am a PhD student in economics on the 2025-2026 academic job market. I study environmental and public economics with a focus on the governance of internationally shared resources. I am a NOAA-SeaGrant fellow in Marine Resource Economics and a doctoral student fellow of the Harvard Environmental Economics Program. I have a B.A. in economics from Harvard University.

My job market paper, <a href="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/papers/deLoera_JMP_fisheries.pdf">**Climate Change and the Common-Pool Problem in Fisheries**</a>, estimates how fisheries extraction is affected by split country control and how this will evolve as climate change shifts the habitable range of many fish populations.
